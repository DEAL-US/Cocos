Pipeline

From raw sequencing reads to resistance interpretation. Start from what you have: each model asks for what it needs, and Cocos runs the steps before it.

  • Assembly

    Assemble the genome from the raw sequencing reads of Illumina (SPAdes) or Nanopore (Raven or Flye).

    InFASTQOutFASTA

  • Annotation

    Annotate the genes of the assembled genome with Bakta.

    InFASTAOutBakta JSON

  • Prediction

    Predict the resistance to each antibiotic with the models you choose, trained or based on expert rules.

    InFASTABakta JSONOutMatrix · CSV

Open and semantic

For researchers and developers
